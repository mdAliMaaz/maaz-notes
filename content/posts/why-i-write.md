## A little mathematics

For a sequence $a_n$, convergence means that the terms get arbitrarily close to a limit $L$:

$$
\forall \varepsilon > 0,\ \exists N\ \text{such that}\ n>N \Rightarrow |a_n-L|<\varepsilon.
$$

That idea is useful far beyond analysis: it is a compact way of thinking about approximation, error, and guarantees.
